# AI FILTERING CRITERIA
# ========================================

## What Makes a Positive News Story?

A positive news story focuses primarily on **improvements to society** in one or more areas:
- Climate
- Entertainment  
- AI & Science & Technology
- Arts & Culture
- Business & Finance
- Politics

## Key Requirements

The article **MUST** have a **clear idea for improvement**. It doesn't need to be purely positive, but must show:

- Progress toward a solution
- Cooperation or agreements
- Breakthroughs or innovations
- New openings or launches
- Reforms or policy improvements
- Aid, protection, or rescue efforts
- Steps forward (even small ones)
- Deals or compromises reached

## Heavy Topics Can Be Positive

Even articles about difficult subjects (war, strikes, protests) qualify if they show **forward movement**:

✅ "Russia and Ukraine Agree to Three-Day Ceasefire" - Progress toward peace
✅ "Writers Guild reaches deal, ending strike" - Resolution achieved  
✅ "New govt in Hungary pledges democratic reforms" - Democratic progress
✅ "ASEAN leaders adopt measures to ease economic pain" - Proactive solutions

## What to REJECT

❌ Pure crisis/disaster with no solutions
❌ Negative events with no improvement angle
❌ Escalating conflicts with no progress
❌ Stories about fears, warnings, threats with no action taken

## Examples of Positive Angles

- "Rent freeze approved" → Housing affordability progress
- "Subway opens in LA" → Infrastructure improvement  
- "Scientists closer to HIV vaccine" → Medical advancement
- "France sends ambassador back to Algeria" → Diplomatic thaw
- "AI banned from Oscars" → Protection of human creativity
- "€1 meals tested at French university" → Educational equity
- "Palestine Marathon held despite conflict" → Resilience and normalcy

## Guiding Principle

Ask: **"Does this show society moving forward in some way?"**

If YES → It's positive news for Rally.
If NO → Reject it.
